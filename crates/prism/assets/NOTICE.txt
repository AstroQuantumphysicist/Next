Rasterized using Pillow/FreeType; glyph coverage and advances transformed into fixed byte arrays for Prism.

The Selawik Regular atlas is derived from the checked-in Selawik source under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see Selawik-OFL.txt. It covers printable ASCII,
printable Latin-1, and the documented punctuation additions at 14/16/20 px.
The guest codepoint map is a project-authored little-endian table; missing
Selawik glyphs use the explicit aliases and visible fallback recorded in
docs/third-party/selawik-font-provenance.json.
